# HAMLET Product Brief

## Product
HAMLET for Your Shortlets

## Parent company
Abundance Abodes

## Market category
Shortlet marketplace + property operating platform

## Mission
Make shortlet ownership easier and shortlet booking safer.

## Brand mantra
Make it easy. Make it trustworthy. Make it accountable.

## Product philosophy
Make the complex feel simple.

## Core value proposition
Shortlets, with accountability.

## Current stage
Founding phase. This is an early market-validation product designed to learn what demand, trust, and operational friction exist in Nigeria's shortlet market before scaling the wider platform.

## Initial market
- Lagos
- Abuja
- Ibadan

## Target users
### Guests
People looking for short-term accommodation for:
- December travel
- weddings and parties
- family visits
- business trips
- diaspora arrivals
- couples and staycations

### Owners and managers
Shortlet owners, property managers, and operators who need better tools to:
- list properties
- manage availability
- handle guest communication
- improve trust and accountability
- understand demand and pricing

### Researchers and founders
People who understand the shortlet business and can help define what the product should become.

## Primary goals
1. Introduce HAMLET clearly and credibly.
2. Build trust without overclaiming.
3. Recruit founding shortlet owners and managers.
4. Capture guest demand, especially around December.
5. Gather structured market research.
6. Create a foundation for the future HAMLET marketplace.

## Positioning principles
- Premium but practical
- Local and culturally aware
- technologically modern
- honest about current stage
- not a generic Airbnb clone
- not a luxury-real-estate template
- not a fake AI startup aesthetic

## Brand personality
- intelligent
- local
- premium
- trustworthy
- human
- confident
- modern
- simple

## Key product signals
- clarity around why a stay is trustworthy
- structured lead capture for both guests and owners
- demand discovery for December travel and events
- founder-facing market learning
- a future AI companion, Horatio, that helps users without inventing facts

## Non-goals for this release
- fake booking inventory
- fake social proof
- fake urgency claims
- fabricated trust statistics
- broad market claims without evidence

## Success metrics for validation
- guest interest submissions
- founding host applications
- December demand intent data
- message quality and repeat pain points from research feedback
- conversion from interest to interviews and qualified conversations

## Product honesty rule
The website should clearly communicate that HAMLET is in its early stage, with emphasis on trust, learning, and meaningful founder conversations rather than inflated product claims.

## Launch intent
The initial landing page is not meant to replace a large marketplace. It is meant to be the first door into a much larger company: a trustworthy, accountable shortlet operating system for Africa.
